# ADR 001 — Do not reanchor potential to GDP

A gap outside about half to a little more than double the calibrated level is a unit or calibration error (A-09). Copying observed GDP into potential hides it and pins inflation, the policy rate and the price index on their ceilings.

The monthly close keeps the calibrated level. The window only reads the published snapshot and shows the discrepancy, the calibration flag and the clamp count.
