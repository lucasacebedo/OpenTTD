# Benchmark B1

Fidelity: F2, the integer aggregate. One worker. Release build.

Map class: 256. Horizon used by the throughput loop: 100000 years, 12 months each.
Pass bar: at least 100000 simulated years per wall second, with the expenditure identity holding on every month.

F0 remains the vehicle game. This measurement does not retarget that simulation. The quantity, price, equilibrium and impulse-response distance between F0 and F2 is printed by FormatFidelityReport during the acceptance test.
